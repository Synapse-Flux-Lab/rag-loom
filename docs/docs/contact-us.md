## Contact Window Benefits (Limited Slots)

1. **Co-design sessions** with our product architects to shape the next wave of retrieval, evaluation, and governance features.
2. **Migration playbooks** tailored to your document estate, whether you are retiring legacy search or augmenting analysts with LLM copilots.
3. **Runway pricing** that locks in preferred rates for the first twelve months of commercial support.

These seats are capped per quarter so that we can stay embedded with each partner. Once the current cohort fills, the next onboarding window shifts by roughly 60 days.
